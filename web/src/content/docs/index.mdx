## What we're building toward

You'll write a program that welcomes the player, asks what they would enjoy doing, and adds weighted points to MISA clusters. It will show their strongest matches at the end. Then you can change a question and let a friend try your version.

You don't need to know Python yet. Bring your laptop, and keep this guide open beside your editor. We have a [browser fallback](./setup/#use-colab-if-setup-gets-in-the-way) if local setup gets in the way.

The finished quiz has 15 questions and no right answers. Each choice gives 2 points to its closest cluster and 1 point to a related cluster. Results show the top three score levels, including ties.

```text title="A sample result"
1. Events | 24.4% of quiz points
You may enjoy turning an idea into an event through program planning, logistics, and dry runs.
2. Human Resources | 20.0% of quiz points
You may enjoy supporting members through community activities, mentorship, and wellbeing work.
```

The percentages describe only the points earned in this quiz. Use the result to start a conversation about MISA, not as a personality assessment or an application result.

## How we'll build it

| Part | What you'll have working |
| --- | --- |
| [Welcome the player](./build/welcome/) | A greeting that asks for the player's name |
| [Ask one question](./build/question/) | A choice that retries when the input is invalid |
| [Loop through answers](./build/loops/) | Seven numbered questions and a score for every cluster |
| [Give your code a job](./build/functions/) | Small functions you can read and reuse |
| [Show your results](./build/results/) | Percentages and tied ranks |
| [Improve your quiz](./build/improve/) | 15 grounded questions with weighted matches |
| [Test and personalize](./build/test/) | Your own quiz, saved in Git |

The whole session is planned for 70 to 90 minutes, including introductions and closing. That's a pacing target; helpers can point you to a working checkpoint when you need one.
